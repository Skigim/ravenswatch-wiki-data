Kintarō and his bear Kuma were sent from their mountain home to deliver a message to the witch Baba Yaga. Kintarō fights wildly, joyfully, and without mercy. Kuma fights because he is very, very hungry.

Together, these brothers from the forest are formidable wardens. But even when dismounted, Kintaro is a ferocious warrior, and Kuma a mountain that moves.
