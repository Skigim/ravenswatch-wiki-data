## The Curse of a Drake's Mother

### Stanza I - The Great Warrior

Skalds call me Beowulf in ballad and song
Ocean spawned fury, a sword-wielding storm
Foul kingdoms I've cowered, their misers laid low
My warriors beside me fear no foe

Great Grendel I'd gutted within his deep grotto
His Mother's sweet poison I drank from her bottle
Sped to her death alongside her son
Where Monsters meet justice, their crimes undone

A weakness, alas, I'd failed to imagine
Would bring my demise and that of my crewmen
Proudly I went to save village defiled
By Nightmare made dragon, by drake made wild

### Stanza II - Drake's Final Breath

We tether our oars in festering fog
Shadows cling fiendish, the dew made sod
The path to the lair is covered in thorns
A wretched vine, withered from witch's scorn

We charge dire drake, with breasts steel-clad
Ice-tempered lances, their tips painted red
Deep in wyrm flesh our fury finds ground
Drake breath-fueled furnace makes meat of my men

When all is o'er, my heart still stutters
My party lays lifeless, swords sunk between scales
The wyrm wisps drowning in beastly ichor
Our blood mingles, its breath grows lighter

Rage receding, I stare in dragon eyes
A Nightmare escapes them and flies to the skies
Amongst the dead bodies of my martyred foray
Out pops a pup, from a clutch of drake eggs

### Stanza III - Beowulf's Bane

Squeezing drake's neck, my hand disobeys
I have slain the pup's mother in fury, in vain
A Nightmare had mastered the drake's willed spirit
Monsters laid claim to innocent victim

For weeks I wander with scaled child in tow
A cur spurning milk, blood its favored brew
Worthy mother of dragon proves hard to find
Monsters are best raised by their own kind

Drakes were in hiding, so I turn to the Watch
The Wardens o' the Raven have monsters to catch
I offer the pup, they offer a test
"Rid Reverie of Nightmares, join our brave quest"

### Stanza IV - Warrior Made Warden

A warden's badge I agree to take
In hopes of redemption for the drake's sake
We fight like the demons I know I've become
Well fit for a saga, unfit to raise sons

I down foul spirits, the pup under wing
Fire his first verses, such sad notes to sing
His mother forgotten, perhaps never known
Ward of Raven, his bosom, his home

None would raise my vile dragon spawn
Each hero, their curse, alone must be borne
None would carry my dreaded guilt
And raise the child of the dragon I'd killed

### Stanza V - A Viking Nursemaid

Bards call me Beowulf, father to drake
Mocking my memory, my foolish mistake
Slaying a dragon and sparing her spawn
Cursed to raise him as my own son

So fighting I teach, my drake a bright pupil
Nightmares we chase though the task is futile
For every beast slain, a second is born
For every soul saved, another is scorned

Sandman calls the Watch with news he's learned
The veil between dream and death is torn
The Witch we once knew, named Baba Yaga
Has carried out this foul deed, her reason uncertain

### Stanza VI - A Warrior Learns Fear

We gather our gear, my curs'd pup and I
The Witch we will slaughter, ensuring she dies
To settle our score, to pay for her curse
I shout: Baba Yaga, come do your worst

Cornered at sunset, the pup spews rage
And charges the sorceress, brave for his age
The Witch casts a spell that singes my drake
I fear she has killed him, my heart nearly breaks

An anger consumes me, I race at the witch
She falls and I leave her to see to my pup
My son is but stunned and still breathes fire
I turn to Baba Yaga, but her body is gone

### Stanza VII - Warden Witchbane

Cradled pup sleeps by my sewn side
He snores, we snore, like waves at high tide
He dreams, we dream, of boasting and battle
He plays with bones, his rage babe's rattle

My curse is lifted, to blessing turned
Yet still I remember my son's mother wyrm
Foul fate, I now teach him to kill or be killed
A peace-filled life forgotten and stilled

Bards call me Beowulf, Warden Witchbane
A Ravenswatch father who fought to tame
The pup of a dragon, and make him his son
Man and Monster, fighting as one
