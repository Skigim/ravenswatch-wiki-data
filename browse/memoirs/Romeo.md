## Thy Letters to Thine Self

### 'Tis here begins your morning named dismay

Dear Romeo, 

Oh Romeo, 'tis thee
who writes these lines with thine own hand.

Ridiculous! I hear thee say. And so 
Thoust speaketh every morn these many moons
since Juliet and thou feigned death and won.

'Tis true your poison ruse was met with great
success. Alas, the price was steep. Thou'st kept
thy wit, but lost come morn its mental purchase.

Heed this early hour to ponder why 
thy written words are nightly lost to mem'ry 
whence thou'st fall to dream, dawn scars attesting
now, that yesterday thy sword was surely 
drawn, thy scratches healed before the dawn.

Be off, now kiss thy Juliet, her heart 
has never faltered, sacred vows she's never 
altered. Daily she remembers all 
since Baba Yaga's curse had last 
transpired. since tragic death you both beguiled.

### Our Family Feud is All the More to Blame

Dear Romeo, again I write to me,

Thee readily shall noteth soon with fright 
how old and tired this letter has become. 
For thou hast read it o'er a thousand times.
Its weathered state bears testament to countless
Frowns, frustrations toll, awrit upon
Thine aging brow, and Juliet's fair face.
So strange a curse to live and then forget.
So strangely blessed revived by Juliet
But as this never ending day grows darker
Thou hast come to doubt, this gift is bent
For if you die your lover dies as well.
So dictates Baba's wicked healing spell.

To blame a witch for all that has befallen
Us, tolls clearer than a morning bell
But truth be known, our present strife is not
the fault of Yaga. Father and our family
Feud is what put turtle doves to flight
And turned our dreams to sleepless frightful nights.
So I implore thee future shadow of 
myself, encourage Juliet with flow'r 
and warming words. She is no more to blame 
than midnight moth aflitting to the flame.

Thine Romeo of Montagues of Family Fought

### A Sword of Fortune Vowed to Ravenswatch

Dear Romeo, bold Romeo lend ear,
 
At last we've read enough to know these letters
Are from thee. And so 'tis time to speak
about this flock of ravens, brash unkindness,
Juliet and thee have opt'd to join.
We wear the emblem of the Ravenswatch.
These warriors know thy name and worth in battle
Tested by their sides in lines hardheld
against vile hordes invading Reverie. 
So now we quell with quill these mem'ries fled.

The warrior braying fiercely with a dragon 
pup in tow we call sir Beowulf.
And he is often found acourting siren
Melusine although the babe fast in
her belly is not his. Important is 
this detail lest you rend their fragile hoax.
Doth not forget Geppetto who we knew
when we were boy. His toys are now quite deadly,
Keep to mind the nightmares slew his child.
The wizard's name I have forgot, the boy
who sits atop a beast is friend not foe.
The flax-haired maiden soft and sweet is far
more deadly than first gaze would wrongly tell.

Thy Romeo of Heralded Ravenswatch

### Eternal Love Expires Sans Mortal Age

Dear Romeo, sad Romeo, shed tears,

A melancholy lurks amidst the glee
a fleeting felon peeking through that smile
our Juliet pretends joyful pity free.
And if perchance thou seest clear beyond
the laughter, past pure love's embrace. Know this,
though we are still a child in mind, and she
a princess grown, for sake of love we must
endure. A child to raise was once a hope
we'd fashioned in our dreams of futures shared.
But now I am that boy and she the elder 
sister of a farce the fates begat.

Unworthy of her mindful patience, dull
where she doth shine. A toddler wielding well
his sabre whilst she learneth twice combined.
If tears of sorrow fill thine eyes dry fast
for Juliet must never fears suspect.
Thou hast the day from dawn 'til sleep requires
its restful due, to chase the child and capt
the man she needs thee quickly to become.
The blessing turns to curse whence we forget
that happiness comes never sans its cost.

Thine Romeo of Drying Eyes

### The Final Quest to Slay the Wretched Witch

Dear Romeo, brave Romeo the bold,

This day thou'st had a vision, how to free
thy Juliet from Charon's chained enchantment
whence thee die so wouldst thy maiden perish.
Death! The caster must endure, for death's
own spell to die, the curse's curser must depart.
Thou art still clever though thy mind is young.
Thou knowst that wither Baba Yaga's spell
has been undone, thy life might forfeit, failing
magic's hold to earthly realms below.
This must be done for Juliet deserves
a life unfettered by thy cursed coil.
The witch, well she must die. Today would be
most fitting for tomorrow never comes.

And now these final words I must address
to thee my sweet, sweet, Juliet.
Forgive me my untelling of our forlorn
paradise. Thou dost deserve a better
end than tied to miscued blasted strife.
If Baba's witchly final breath undoes
my life anon. Then know I leave this stage
with no regret for I have loved. And I've
been loved. And love my soul has wed.

Romeo Montagues of Rightful Quest

### The Witch Is Found the Bullet Buried Deep

Dear Romeo of Witchly Battle Wrought,

The plan was simple, carefully thought, to slay
a witch and make her not, and so we charged
two Lancelots entwined in deathly dance
we fought, to down the nightmare sorceress.
Mad Baba Yaga fought with rage from flying 
cauldron, boiling bile and spitting flames.
With each and every step we took the witch
had counter, Juliet went down to knee.
Thou jumped and jabbed within the secret second
thine own lady had prepared. A trap 
of Capulet device, her plan, your hand.

Within that fateful glimpse of triumph, thou 
didst shatter all resolve to fell the cursed
Architect of thine calamity. 
Forsooth the witch's ghastly plans must end,
Alas, thy life must carry on so long 
as Juliet draws breath. She draws upon
thy youthful hope to fire her guns on sight.
And thou reliest upon her wit to carry 
Thee 'til night, unto the morning light.
The witch's life we spared, her monsters on
the run. Our deadly dance has just begun.

Thy Romeo wed Capulet of Battle Won

### No Love Has Worth Lest Rife with Hesitation

Oh Romeo, true Romeo, at last,

Embrace the joy this danger-filled today prepares
For though you will forget the details, bonds
of friendship in the Ravenswatch remain.
It's seen within these warriors' gazes, trust
is not a prize unearned, but given only
After proof of loyalty is learnt.
For thou hast found eventful purpose side
by side with Juliet. The life you two 
have forged from sorrow, tempered nightly in
the waters of the Styx, is chance for thee
to love delightful fired by newness, wonder
ever kindling kisses stolen from the grave.

'Tis also chance for her to grow and ponder, 
brightly thinking, smarter grinning, ever 
fonder of the man thou hast become. 
There is no shame for two sound souls to walk 
as one. Each day its share of novelty.
Each night its share of unexpected love. 
I lift a glass to future dawns when children
born from blessed compromise, will call
to me their father mindful I've forgotten
all their names but not the love I store.

Mine Romeo of Lifted Heart
